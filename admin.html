Help

Daily routine

  1. Today tab: check the day's bookings. The page refreshes by itself every couple of minutes, or press Refresh. New bookings from the website appear within about 5 minutes.
  2. Waivers & vaccinations tab: open the records waiting for review, press View file to check the vet record (right dog, right vaccine, date not expired), then Approve or Reject.
  3. Extra-time requests (bottom of Today): Approve or Decline. If you approve, also lengthen that appointment in Google Calendar by hand.
  4. Customers & dogs tab: search by name, email, phone or dog's name. Red means something is missing or expired.

Common questions

How do I add a team member? Open the Staff accounts tab (admins only), fill in Add a team member, and press Add person. Give them the temporary password shown on screen. They must also click the confirmation link emailed to them once.

Someone on the team forgot their password. In Staff accounts, press Send password reset next to their name.

Someone has left the team. In Staff accounts, change their role to customer and press Save. They lose staff access straight away.

Where can I see a dog's photo? In the Dog gallery tab, and next to each dog in Customers & dogs and in today's bookings. Customers add and change their dog's photo from their own account page.

A customer forgot their password. Ask them to open the sign-in page and click “Forgot your password?”.

A customer says their upload failed. Files must be a PDF or photo (JPG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC) of 5 MB or less. They can also bring the record to the store.

A booking looks wrong or missing. The calendar is the master copy. Fix it in Google Calendar and it will update here within about 5 minutes.

Who can see what? Customers see only their own information. Staff see all customers. Only admins can change roles or download the backup.

Owner notes (admins only)

Waiver: legal review. It is recommended that a lawyer licensed in British Columbia reviews the waiver before you rely on it.

Electronic signature. Customers sign by typing their full name. Confirm with your legal adviser that this method meets your operational and legal requirements.

Backups. The free database has no automatic backups. Download the CSV from the Staff accounts tab regularly, and keep it somewhere private.

Keep the database awake. Free Supabase projects pause after about a week of very low activity. The calendar sync that runs every 5 minutes helps prevent this. If you ever receive a “project will be paused” email, open the Supabase dashboard once.

Secrets. The Supabase secret key lives only in the Apps Script project’s Script properties. Never put it on GitHub or send it to anyone.

Practice data. Fake “Training” customers can be removed with the 04_remove_training_data.sql script.
